Nobody has actually asked me most of these questions. But if enough people exist in the world, someone has thought them. This page is for those people.

## General Questions

> Were you born in August?

No, September. The name is a family thing. I've been explaining this my entire life and I expect to continue doing so.

> Who were you named after?

My great grandfather Weldon "Gus" August Ploger. I never met him. By all accounts he was a character. I choose to take that as a compliment on his behalf and mine.

> How do you pronounce your last name?

GID-ree. It's Cajun.

> Where are you from?

New Orleans, Louisiana. Specifically the kind of New Orleans that people from New Orleans recognize, not the kind that ends up on a travel blog.

> Do you have a wife?

No, but I'd like one. Applications are open. This is not a bit.

> Do you have any children?

No. See above.

> Are you related to [XYZ] Guidry?

Almost certainly not. Guidry is one of the most common surnames in Louisiana. We are a large and distributed people. I wish your Guidry well.

> Myers–Briggs Type Indicator

INTJ. Make of that what you will. I certainly have.

> Tabs or spaces?

**Tabs**. Spaces are a moral failing.

> Vi or Emacs?

**Vi**. I don't have anything against Emacs users personally. I just don't think we'd get along.

## Favorites

### Manga

**Akira** by Katsuhiro Otomo (1982 – 1990). If you haven't read it, fix that. If you've only seen the movie, the manga will make you feel like you've been living in a basement.

### Movie

[**_Blade Runner_**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner) by Ridley Scott (1982). I also am a fan of the book it was based on — _Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?_ by Philip K. Dick (1968). The movie and the book are asking different questions. Both are worth your time.

### Video Game

[**_Star Wars Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords_**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_II:_The_Sith_Lords) (2004) is the best Star Wars installment and video game of all time. This title could never be made in the current era due to the mature themes surrounding the story.
