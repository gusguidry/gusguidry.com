A curated page of sites I find myself returning to or recommending. Inspired by the old-school tradition of the personal links page.

## Blogs and Personal Sites

- [Kottke](https://kottke.org) — The original link blogger, still going.
- [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net) — Links and notes on AI, open source, and the web.
- [Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net) — John Gruber on Apple, tech, and writing.
- [NeoCities](https://neocities.org) — A revival of the personal web.

## Tools I Actually Use

- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) — Hand-drawn diagramming, no account needed.
- [Coolors](https://coolors.co) — A fast color palette generator.
- [Typescale](https://typescale.com) — Preview type scales before committing to one.

## Interesting Corners of the Web

- [The Pudding](https://pudding.cool) — Visual essays done right.
- [Marginalia Search](https://search.marginalia.nu) — A search engine that favors the non-commercial web.
- [Are.na](https://are.na) — Visual bookmarking, quieter than the rest.

## Reading

- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) — The best comment section on the internet (sometimes).
- [Longform](https://longform.org) — Curated long-form journalism.
