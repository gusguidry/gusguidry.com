The best way to keep up with this site is a feed reader. You subscribe once, and every new post lands there in full the moment it's published. I never get your email address, and no algorithm decides whether you see it.

## The feeds

<p class="feed-links">
  <a href="/feed.xml"><img class="social-icon" src="/images/rss.svg" alt="">RSS</a>
  <a href="/atom.xml"><img class="social-icon" src="/images/atom.svg" alt="">Atom</a>
</p>

Same posts either way, full text, nothing held back to make you click through. If you don't know which to pick, pick RSS. If you have opinions about syndication formats, you already know you want Atom.

Most readers will also find the feed on their own if you just paste in `gusguidry.com`.

## Just one topic

Every tag has its own feed. Add `feed.xml` to the end of any tag page, like [`/tags/opinion/feed.xml`](/tags/opinion/feed.xml), and you'll only get posts on that subject.

## 日本語

Japanese posts have their own feeds: [RSS](/ja/feed.xml) and [Atom](/ja/atom.xml).

## Don't have a reader?

[NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com) is free and excellent on Mac and iPhone. [Feedly](https://feedly.com) works anywhere with a browser. Either one will do; the point is that you choose what you read, not a timeline.
